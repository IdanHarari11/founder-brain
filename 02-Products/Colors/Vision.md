# Colors — Vision

## Mission
Connect candidates, recruiters, businesses, and freelance HR professionals in one AI-powered marketplace.

## Target Market
Israeli recruitment market. Hebrew-first, expanding.

## Revenue Model
- Recruiter/Company: Subscription tiers (free → starter → basic → premium → pro → admin)
- Candidate: free / premium (₪29.90/mo)
- Freelancer: regular (₪149/mo) / premium (₪249/mo)
- Business: Own subscription schema

## Key Differentiators
- 3-stage AI matching pipeline (hard gates → scoring → deep analysis)
- Asynchronous matching via BullMQ (never blocks UI)
- Multi-role marketplace (not just recruiter↔candidate)
- Freelancer HR professionals as first-class role

## Stage
Core flows live. Freelancer role is the next major gap.
